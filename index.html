What this is

We opened Hushed the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what actually moves conversion and revenue across the market, not inside one app. We pointed that at Hushed: installed it, signed up, picked a number, read your App Store listing and hushed.com, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people before they pay.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: your wine and coral, the H badge, the serif number, your plan cards with the 3-day trial bar, your white “don’t leave it behind” sheet. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
